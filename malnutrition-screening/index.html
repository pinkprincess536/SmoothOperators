Malnutrition screening

WHO LMS z-scores, MUAC, and combined acute classification — works offline after first load.

Measurements

WHZ uses length +0.7 cm correction for children under 24 months (same convention as standard field tables).

How to measure
  • Midpoint between the tip of the shoulder and the tip of the elbow
  • Arm hanging relaxed at the side
  • Use a non-stretch tape; read to the nearest mm
  • MUAC cut-offs used here apply to ages 6–59 months only

    Research & validation notes

    National surveys such as NFHS and DHS publish prevalence estimates using the same underlying WHO growth standards, but agreement with this app requires identical LMS tables, age definitions, and measurement protocols. MUAC-for-age reference curves exist in some research settings; this build uses fixed MUAC thresholds (115 / 125 mm) for 6–59 months and real-time measurement input, as is common in acute screening.

    Clinical decisions require trained assessment. This tool supports screening, not diagnosis.
